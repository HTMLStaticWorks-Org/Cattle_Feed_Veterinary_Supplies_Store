LIVESTOCK WELLBEING

Care is part of every good routine

Consistent observation, suitable nutrition and timely support all play a role.

Every herd is different. Use this page as a general starting point and consult a qualified veterinarian for diagnosis, treatment and animal-health decisions.

OBSERVE · PREVENT · SUPPORT

Pay attention to the small signs.

Regularly observe feeding, movement, body condition and changes in behaviour. Early attention can help you decide when professional advice is needed.

  • Keep fresh water available
  • Maintain clean, comfortable spaces
  • Follow appropriate feeding guidance
  • Seek veterinary advice when animals seem unwell

CARE AREAS

A few essentials to keep in focus

Young animal care

Give calves a clean, safe environment and follow suitable feeding guidance.

Dairy routines

Keep milking spaces and equipment clean and follow farm hygiene procedures.

Herd observation

Watch for changes in appetite, movement and general behaviour, and seek qualified advice when something seems unusual.

A ROUTINE THAT ADAPTS

Observe the animal. Improve the environment. Ask for help early.

Good livestock care is a series of consistent checks rather than a single task. Notice changes in appetite, movement and behaviour, keep housing and water points clean, and follow feeding guidance suited to the animal and its stage of life.

  • 01Observe dailyLook for changes in appetite, movement and general condition.
  • 02Maintain the spaceKeep bedding, feeding areas and water points clean and comfortable.
  • 03Get qualified adviceContact a veterinarian when an animal appears unwell or needs treatment.
