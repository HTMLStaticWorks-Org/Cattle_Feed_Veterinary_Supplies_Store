BEFORE YOU SEND A MESSAGE

Quick answers to help you get started.

These notes explain what to include in your enquiry and where FarmCare can help. For animal diagnosis or treatment, please speak with a qualified veterinarian.

View all FAQs
What details should I include in my enquiry?+

Tell us your product category, animal type or farm requirement. If you know the quantity or preferred timeframe, include that too.

Can I ask about availability and bulk quantities?+

Yes. Mention the item, approximate quantity and your location so the team can respond with the most relevant information.

Does FarmCare provide veterinary diagnosis?+

No. FarmCare provides general product and supply information. Diagnosis, treatment and urgent animal-health decisions should be handled by a qualified veterinary professional.

How will you respond to my message?+

Use the email address or phone number shown on this page. The demonstration form needs a real email or server integration before it can deliver messages.
